## Mechanism (why large-distance images)
BSSP stops at ||adv - cln|| < tol = 1e-4 (normalised space), i.e. an angular resolution of tol/d_n. For d_n ~ 240-376 (pixel L2 55-85 x 4.42) this is ~3e-7 rad, at the float32 limit for direction arithmetic on 150528-dim unit vectors; the bisection collapses (directions equal) before tol is met and the dot rounds to 1 + 2^-23. Small-distance victims (vgg19, vit) never reach this regime. Consistent with all observed stall/no-stall cells (inference, not separately tested).

## Disagreements with B4d
1. T1 precision: baselines run cuDNN TF32 on (torch default), ours FP32 (frozen engine). Not a reproducibility issue but an oracle-parity issue: hard-label outputs end within ~tol of the boundary, so adv_float (D3) and attack dynamics are evaluated on numerically different classifiers. Recommend D12: frozen-engine flags for all adapters.
2. T3 metric: wall-clock with imbalanced workloads; the right measure is weighted speedup S = sum_p (solo s/img / concurrent s/img). In the contended window EGC did 5 images while TtBA did ~1 (122 s vs 38 s solo): S ~ 1.1. Verdict (solo for TtBA+EGC) stands; TtBA+TtBA had 1.72x in B4.
